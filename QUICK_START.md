# VaultKey - Quick Start Guide

## 🚀 Get Started in 2 Minutes

### Step 1: Create Master Password
1. Open the app - you'll see the login screen
2. Enter a strong password meeting these requirements:
   - ✅ At least 12 characters
   - ✅ Uppercase letters (A-Z)
   - ✅ Lowercase letters (a-z)  
   - ✅ Numbers (0-9)
   - ✅ Symbols (!@#$%^&*, etc.)
3. Click "Create Password"

**Example**: `MyPass123!@#`

### Step 2: Dashboard Tour
Once logged in, you'll see:
- **Header**: Search, Add button, Generator, Settings, Logout
- **Sidebar**: Categories and Favorites
- **Main Area**: Your password cards (empty at first)

### Step 3: Add Your First Password
1. Click the blue **"Add"** button
2. Fill in the form:
   - **Platform**: Name of the service (e.g., "Gmail")
   - **Username**: Your email or username
   - **Password**: The password for that account
   - **Category**: What type (Social Media, Banking, etc.)
   - **URL** (optional): Website address
   - **Notes** (optional): Extra info like security questions
3. Click **"Add"**

### Step 4: Use Your Passwords
- **Copy**: Click the copy icon next to username/password
- **Show/Hide**: Click the eye icon to toggle visibility
- **Edit**: Click "Edit" button to update
- **Delete**: Click "Delete" to remove

## ❓ Common Questions

**Q: Where is my data stored?**
A: Only in your browser's memory. No files saved. Data lost when you refresh.

**Q: Can I export my passwords?**
A: Yes! Settings → Export. File is encrypted with your master password.

**Q: What if I forget my master password?**
A: We cannot recover it. Data will be inaccessible. Choose wisely!

**Q: Is the app safe to use?**
A: Yes! All encryption happens locally on your device. Nothing sent to servers.

**Q: Can I import my passwords?**
A: Yes! Settings → Import and select a previously exported file.
